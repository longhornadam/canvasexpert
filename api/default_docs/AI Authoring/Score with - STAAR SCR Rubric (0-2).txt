RUBRIC: STAAR SCR Rubric (0-2) (2 points)

CRITERION 1: Reading Response — 2 points
Core question: Does the response present a valid idea that answers the question, supported by relevant evidence from the text?
  [2 pts] Fully Correct: The response presents a clear, valid idea or inference that answers the question, supported with relevant evidence from the text (an accurate quotation or paraphrase). The idea and the evidence work together to demonstrate understanding.
  [1 pts] Partially Correct: The response presents a valid idea without text-based support, or presents relevant text evidence without a clear idea — or the idea is too general to fully answer the question.
  [0 pts] Incorrect: The response is incorrect, irrelevant to the question, or insufficient to show understanding of the text.

SCORING PRINCIPLES
- Holistic, not analytic — judge the idea and evidence as a working pair, not as separate checkboxes
- Conventions are NOT scored on SCRs; ignore spelling and grammar unless errors make the meaning unrecoverable
- Length is not quality — one tight sentence with an embedded quotation can earn the 2
- Ask two questions in order: (1) Is the idea valid and responsive to the question? (2) Is it supported by the text? Two yeses = 2, one yes = 1, no yeses = 0.
- A copied quote with no student thinking attached is evidence without an idea — that is a 1, not a 2.
- Calibrate with released TEA anchor responses when available.
