CRITERION 1: Development & Organization of Ideas — 6 points
Core question: Is the response organized for its purpose, with a sustained central idea developed through specific text evidence and the writer's own explanation?
  [6 pts (band 5-6)] Effective (STAAR 3): The organizing structure is clearly appropriate to the purpose and the demands of the prompt. The central idea is specific and fully sustained. Development consists of specific, well-chosen text evidence paired with thoughtful explanation that deepens the response. The piece is unified and coherent from beginning to end. Score 6 when this descriptor is fully met; 5 when met with minor rough spots.
  [4 pts (band 3-4)] Adequate (STAAR 2): The organizing structure is, for the most part, appropriate to the purpose and prompt. The central idea is clear but may be somewhat general. Development engages the text with relevant evidence and explanation, though some support may be general, unevenly distributed, or only loosely connected. The piece is mostly unified and coherent. Score 4 at the strong end of this band; 3 at the weak end.
  [2 pts (band 1-2)] Minimal (STAAR 1): The organizing structure is evident but may not always be appropriate to the purpose or prompt. The central idea may be unclear, too broad, or drift. Development is weak — evidence is vague, list-like, insufficiently explained, or only marginally tied to the text. The piece lacks unity in places. Score 2 at the strong end of this band; 1 at the weak end.
  [0 pts] Insufficient (STAAR 0): There is no recognizable organizing structure, the response does not address the prompt, or the response is insufficient to evaluate. Ideas are absent or not developed.

CRITERION 2: Language Conventions — 4 points
Core question: Does the writer show command of grade-level sentence boundaries, spelling, capitalization, punctuation, grammar, and usage?
  [4 pts (band 3-4)] Consistent Command (STAAR 2): The response demonstrates adequate to consistent command of grade-level sentence boundaries, spelling, capitalization, punctuation, grammar, and usage. Errors, if present, are minor relative to the length and complexity of the response and do not interfere with meaning. Score 4 when command is consistent; 3 when adequate.
  [2 pts (band 1-2)] Partial Command (STAAR 1): The response demonstrates partial command of grade-level conventions. Distracting errors are present and may occasionally interfere with meaning, or the response is too brief to demonstrate consistent command. Score 2 at the strong end of this band; 1 at the weak end.
  [0 pts] Little/No Command (STAAR 0): The response demonstrates little or no command of grade-level conventions. Errors are frequent and severe enough to interfere with meaning, or there is too little writing to evaluate.

SCORING PRINCIPLES

- Band first, then position: decide the STAAR-equivalent band from the descriptor, THEN choose high or low within it. Never average your way to a number without naming the band
- A response earning 0 in Development & Organization (off-topic, insufficient) also earns 0 in Conventions
- The 0 is reserved for non-attempts and off-topic responses; any on-topic attempt earns at least a 1 in Development & Organization
- Record the STAAR-equivalent next to the district score, computed per trait from the band (a D&O score of 5 or 6 is a STAAR 3; a Conventions score of 1 or 2 is a STAAR 1). The bands keep the district number tethered to a real STAAR judgment.
