How it keeps data safe

PowerGrader is designed to be FERPA-conscious: student names and obvious PII are separated locally before any AI packet is created.

  • Grade Myself keeps grading local and does not send an AI packet.
  • Use My AI Chat creates a Safe AI Packet with pseudonyms and a private decoder that stays on this computer.
  • Auto-Score With API sends the same anonymized packet through OpenRouter, then reattaches real identities locally.

Rubrics, AI persona instructions, and source excerpts may be included as grading context. Review source material before sending, and avoid pasting unnecessary student identifiers or full copyrighted books.
